gesetzlich nicht vorgesehenen Eintragungen große Zurückhaltung geboten.
Insbesondere darf dadurch das Handelsregister nicht unübersichtlich werden
oder zu Missverständnissen Anlass geben [...].
#+end_quote

/Translation./ It is recognised that a fact can be registrable even
without statutory provision [...]. The Commercial Register has the task of
disclosing the essential legal relationships of traders and enterprises
and of giving correct information on facts legally relevant in commerce
(the publicity function [...]). This is not to be understood as a
guarantee of completeness: the register cannot give a complete picture of
traders and enterprises, if only because it would otherwise become
confusing and could not fulfil its function of providing quick and
intelligible information [...]. Only within these limits can certain facts
be regarded as registrable without statutory provision, by interpretation

of overloading the Commercial Register must give way. There must

* II.2 f): the executorship decision does not carry over

#+begin_quote
Aus der Entscheidung des BGH zur Eintragungsfähigkeit eines
Testamentsvollstreckervermerks bei Dauertestamentsvollstreckung über den
Nachlass eines Kommanditisten [...] lässt sich die Eintragungsfähigkeit
eines Nießbrauchs an einem Kommanditanteil nicht ableiten.

aa) Als maßgeblich für die Eintragungsfähigkeit eines
Testamentsvollstreckervermerks hat der BGH angesehen, dass der
Testamentsvollstrecker [...] materiellrechtlich Inhaber der
gesamthänderischen Verfügungsbefugnis ist. [...] Insoweit entfaltet die
Testamentsvollstreckung eine unmittelbare haftungsrechtliche Außenwirkung.
[...]

bb) Insoweit besteht ein wesentlicher Unterschied zwischen Nießbrauch und
Testamentsvollstreckung. Der Nießbrauch entfaltet weder eine
haftungsrechtliche Außenwirkung noch stehen dem Nießbraucher die einem
Testamentsvollstrecker vergleichbaren Rechte zu. Sofern hinsichtlich eines
Kommanditanteils Testamentsvollstreckung angeordnet ist, muss ein
Gesellschaftsgläubiger, der den Kommanditisten [...] persönlich in
Anspruch nehmen will, den Testamentsvollstrecker als Partei kraft Amtes
verklagen. Gerade dieser Umstand rechtfertigt die Eintragung eines
Testamentsvollstreckervermerks ins Handelsregister [...]. Eine solche, dem
Testamentsvollstrecker vergleichbare materiellrechtliche Stellung hat der
Nießbraucher nicht inne bzw. es besteht bei der Bestellung des Nießbrauchs
keine vergleichbare prozessuale Lage.

cc) Da somit regelmäßig weder im Hinblick auf die Vertretungsverhältnisse
noch im Hinblick auf die Haftungsverteilung ein signifikanter Einfluss des
Nießbrauchers besteht, ist nicht ersichtlich, worin ein unabweisbares
Interesse des gesamten Rechtsverkehrs an der Eintragung des Nießbrauchs an
einem Kommanditanteil begründet sein soll.
#+end_quote

/Translation./ The registrability of a usufruct on a limited partner's
interest cannot be derived from the BGH's decision on the registrability
of a note of executorship for long-term administration of a limited
partner's estate [...].

aa) The BGH regarded as decisive that the executor [...] holds, in
substantive law, the joint power of disposal. [...] To that extent the
executorship has a direct external effect on liability. [...]

bb) In this respect there is an essential difference between usufruct
and executorship. The usufruct has no external effect on liability, nor
does the usufructuary have rights comparable to an executor's. Where an
executorship over a limited partner's interest is ordered, a creditor of
the partnership who wants to claim against the limited partner
personally [...] must sue the executor as a party by virtue of office. It
is precisely this circumstance that justifies entering a note of
executorship [...]. The usufructuary holds no comparable substantive
position, and creating a usufruct gives rise to no comparable procedural
situation.

cc) Since, as a rule, the usufructuary has no significant influence
either on representation or on the distribution of liability, it is not
apparent what could ground a compelling interest of legal dealings as a
whole in entering the usufruct.

* II.2 g): an interest in information does not suffice; clarity first

#+begin_quote
Es besteht nach Auffassung des Senats auch kein erhebliches praktisches
Bedürfnis und kein über ein allgemeines Informationsinteresse

/Translation./ In the senate's view there is also no substantial
practical need, and no substantial interest of legal dealings beyond a
general interest in information, for such an entry [...].

aa) If one held the usufruct registrable by reference to a compelling
need, such a compelling need would have to be affirmed generally for all
facts in whose information legal dealings have a substantial interest.
Information on a partner's loss of legal capacity or on a partner's
guardianship would then be as interesting to commerce as an executorship
over a GmbH share or the "deputy" addition for deputy managing directors
of a GmbH. Yet none of these facts is registrable, on the BGH's case law
too, although legal dealings' interest in knowing of them is undeniable
[...].

(1) [the "deputy" addition, BGH NJW 1998, 1071] [...]

(2) In its decision of 24.2.2015 [...] the BGH holds that a note of
executorship is not among the particulars the statute provides for in a
list of shareholders [...]. It does not suffice for including further
particulars that they may be "useful" information for third parties'
further inquiries. [...]

bb) In this sense, the Commercial Register cannot be a general
collection point for information on traders and commercial companies. It
may contain only entries of essential importance to legal and commercial
dealings, in particular particulars of representation and liability. The
register must be clearly ordered, transparent and intelligible. The
principle of clarity of the register therefore, as a rule, takes
precedence over legal and commercial dealings' interest in comprehensive
information about traders and commercial companies. Accordingly, there
is no room for entering a usufruct on a limited partner's interest.
