* (a) Refutation only: München against the liability ground

The restraint stands unless a need is shown. The appellant offers
liability, and the court refutes it. With the ground gone, the owed need
is unmet and the exception fails.

#+begin_src fspy
debate con closed frag_a : Registrable [usufruct].
muc_restraint.
undermine excl_needs_no_need muc_restraint.
support app_need_liab excl_needs_no_need.
support app_liab app_need_liab.
attack muc_no_liab app_liab.
cedat tempus.
#+end_src

Verdict (stable, 2026-11-14): a value, the refutation of =Registrable=.
Its normal form rests on =!IN:Excluded usufruct=, the presumed exclusion.

AI NOTE (claim): the "refutation only" pattern (IRIS draft § 2.2.1) is a
burden allocation. The restraint is presumed and the need is owed by the
applicant, so a court that only rebuts the grounds offered does decide
the case. AIDA makes the allocation explicit as which sites are
delegations and which are obligations.

AI NOTE (claim):
- München does not reject reasoning by analogy. It rejects the

  depends on another disputed question: liability.

Verdict (stable, 2026-11-14): skeptically open, =?UNDEC:CoDetermines

AI NOTE (claim):
- Köln rebuts constitutive effect, which Oldenburg never claimed.
  Oldenburg's inference from the mere consent right to influence is met
  only by Köln's presumption that there is no influence.
- Two presumed legal readings meet at one statement: grounded and
  skeptically undecided, two stable labellings.
- AIDA localises the disagreement between the decisions to this
  statement.
