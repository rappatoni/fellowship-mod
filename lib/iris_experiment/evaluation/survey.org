* What was compared, and why these

The plan named the frameworks: Carneades, PROLEG, s(CASP), Defeasible
Deontic Logic, and abstract/structured tools (ASPARTIX on clingo, ASPIC+
through TOAST or PyArg, Arg2P). They are the systems the IRIS draft's
community uses for legal argumentation. Each is compared on the same
material (=comparison.org=, section "Material").

* The frameworks, briefly, with sources

AI NOTE: the citations below were checked on 2026-11-14 where marked
(✓). The others are standard citations that were not re-checked.

- *Carneades.* Arguments pro and con a statement, grouped into issues, are
  weighed under a proof standard (preponderance of evidence, clear and
  convincing evidence, beyond reasonable doubt). Premises are ordinary
  premises, assumptions or exceptions, which is where the model places
  the burden of proof. Version 4 adds cyclic graphs and weighing
  functions.
  - T. F. Gordon, H. Prakken, D. Walton, "The Carneades model of argument
    and burden of proof", Artificial Intelligence 171(10-15), 2007,
    875-896.
  - Carneades 4: https://github.com/carneades/carneades-4 (MPL-2.0).
  - The DIREGA group already has a Carneades map of the usufruct case
    (=DIREGA/formalizations/carneades/nießbrauch.yml=).
- *ASPIC+.* Arguments are built from a knowledge base (axioms, ordinary
  premises) with strict and defeasible rules. They attack each other by
  undermining, rebutting and undercutting, and preferences turn attacks
  into defeats. The result is a Dung framework.
  - S. Modgil, H. Prakken, "A general account of argumentation with
    preferences", Artificial Intelligence 195, 2013, 361-397.
  - PyArg: A. Borg, D. Odekerken, "PyArg for solving and explaining
    argumentation in Python: demonstration", COMMA 2022; D. Odekerken,
    A. Borg, M. Berthold, "Demonstrating PyArg 2.0", COMMA 2023 (✓ for
    the names and the package).
- *Arg2P.* An ASPIC+-like engine on tuProlog, with burdens of persuasion:
  a statement that carries the burden and stays undecided is labelled
  OUT (two-stage labelling).
  - R. Calegari, A. Omicini, G. Pisano, G. Sartor, "Arg2P: an
    argumentation framework for explainable intelligent systems", Journal
    of Logic and Computation 32(2), 2022, 369-401,
    doi:10.1093/logcom/exab089 (✓).
  - R. Calegari, G. Sartor, "A model for the burden of persuasion in
    argumentation", JURIX 2020, FAIA 334, 13-22; also "Burden of
    persuasion in argumentation", ICLP 2020, EPTCS 325, 151-163 (✓).
- *s(CASP).* Answer set programming with constraints, evaluated top down
  without grounding, so programs keep their variables. Negation as
  failure gives defaults, classical negation gives refutations, and the
  answer comes with a justification tree.
  - J. Arias, M. Carro, E. Salazar, K. Marple, G. Gupta, "Constraint
    answer set programming without grounding", Theory and Practice of
    Logic Programming 18(3-4), 2018, 337-354.
  - The external importer =../scasp-aida= translates s(CASP) programs to
    AIDA (helper atoms per conjunction).
- *Defeasible Deontic Logic.* Defeasible logic (strict and defeasible
  rules, defeaters, a superiority relation, ambiguity blocking or
  propagating) extended with obligations, permissions and compensations.
  - G. Antoniou, D. Billington, G. Governatori, M. J. Maher,
    "Representation results for defeasible logic", ACM TOCL 2(2), 2001,
    255-287.
  - G. Governatori, F. Olivieri, A. Rotolo, S. Scannapieco, "Computing
    strong and weak permissions in defeasible logic", Journal of
    Philosophical Logic 42(6), 2013, 799-829.
  - The clingo implementation: https://github.com/gvdgdo/Defeasible-Deontic-Logic.
  - Houdini (M. Cristani, G. Governatori, F. Olivieri et al.) is
    described in CEUR-WS Vol-3354 (2022) and Procedia Computer Science
    (2023), but no download was found (✓ for the absence, 2026-11-14).
- *PROLEG.* Legal rules as Prolog rules with exceptions, implementing the
  Japanese "presupposed ultimate fact" theory: the split into rule and
  exception allocates the burden of proof.
  - K. Satoh et al., "PROLEG: an implementation of the presupposed
    ultimate fact theory of Japanese civil code by PROLOG technology",
    JSAI-isAI 2010 workshops, 153-164 (✓ for the citation; no source code
    was found).
- *Abstract argumentation, ASP encodings.*
  - U. Egly, S. A. Gaggl, S. Woltran, "Answer-set programming encodings
    for argumentation frameworks", Argument & Computation 1(2), 2010,
    147-177.
  - ASPARTIX: https://dbai.tuwien.ac.at/proj/argumentation/systempage/dung.html
  - Bipolar frameworks (cited by the IRIS draft): C. Cayrol,
    M.-C. Lagasquie-Schiex, "Bipolar abstract argumentation systems", in
    Argumentation in Artificial Intelligence, 2009, 65-84. ASPARTIX's
    published Dung encodings have no support relation, so supports are
    compiled away by the structured layer (ASPIC+) before the solver sees
    the framework.

* How each tool was installed (2026-11-14, macOS 27 arm64)

All tools were installed outside the repository, each in its own
directory; nothing was installed system-wide.

| Tool | Steps | Notes |
|------+-------+-------|
| Carneades 4 | Go 1.22.12 tarball from go.dev; =git clone https://github.com/carneades/carneades-4=; =go build -ldflags=-linkmode=external ./src/cmd/carneades= | the default Go linker's binary lacks LC_UUID, which macOS 27's loader requires; external linking adds it |
| PyArg, clingo | =python3 -m venv=; =pip install clingo python-argumentation= | PyArg's own stable-extension routine took 166 s on rq7-a (32 arguments, 93 defeats); the runner computes stable extensions of PyArg's framework with clingo and ASPARTIX's stable.dl instead (0.2 s for everything) |
| ASPARTIX | =stable.dl=, =comp.dl=, =condLit_ground.lp=, =prefex_gringo.lp= from the system page | used with clingo's Python API |
| Arg2P | =uv venv --python 3.12=; =uv pip install arg2p= | under Python 3.15 its dependency JPype1 does not build |
| s(CASP) | already installed (Ciao-based, 0.25.12.04) | does not accept named anonymous variables such as =_F= |
| DDL | =git clone https://github.com/gvdgdo/Defeasible-Deontic-Logic= | =deontic.asp= alone has unsafe rules under clingo 5.8.3; =deontic-comp.asp= (the one its own example uses) works |
| PROLEG core | Ciao Prolog 1.25.0-m1, already installed | written for this comparison, =proleg/proleg.pl= |
