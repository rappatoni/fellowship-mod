AI DRAFT (Phase 2 of task =aida-iris-usufruct=). Every RQ 3 document
tangles this file first (=#+fspy_prelude: vocabulary.org=). Statements
are therefore the same propositions across the four decisions, and the
courts' arguments meet in one document graph by proposition identity.

Logic: quantifier-free predicate logic over constants (R1, 2026-11-13).
- One sort, =Fact=: a fact (legal relationship) that someone applies to
  have entered.
- The predicates are properties of the legal position the fact creates.
  For example, =Liability [usufruct]= reads "the usufruct makes its
  holder liable towards third parties".
- General principles that a court argues about as such (by reductio,
  for example) are propositional atoms.

* Individuals

| Constant              | The fact                                                                                   |
|-----------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =usufruct=            | the usufruct on a limited partner's interest, the fact applied for in all four cases       |
| =executorship=        | long-term administration by an executor of a limited partner's estate (BGH, 14.2.2012, II ZB 15/11: registrable) |
| =deputy_note=         | the "deputy" addition to a managing director's entry (BGH NJW 1998, 1071: not registrable)  |
| =gmbh_executorship=   | a note of executorship in a GmbH's list of shareholders (BGH, 24.2.2015: not admissible)     |

#+begin_src fspy
lk.
declare Fact : type.
declare usufruct, executorship, deputy_note, gmbh_executorship : Fact.
#+end_src

* Predicates

| Predicate              | =P [f]= reads                                                                                   |
|------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =Registrable=          | f is registrable (eintragungsfähig)                                                              |
| =ProvidedByStatute=    | the statute provides for f's entry                                                              |
| =Need=                 | there is a need of legal dealings for f's entry, sufficient for registration. The courts word the threshold differently ("unabweisbar", "erheblich", "berechtigtes Interesse"); see =analysis.org= |
| =Excluded=             | the principle of restraint excludes f from the register (helper of the meta-norm, below)         |
| =Liability=            | f makes its holder liable towards third parties                                                  |
| =Representation=       | f makes its holder take part in representing the partnership                                     |
| =VotesDayToDay=        | f gives its holder a vote of their own on day-to-day affairs                                     |
| =ConsentConstitutive=  | the holder's consent under § 1071 BGB conditions the validity of the partners' measure           |
| =Consent1071=          | measures affecting f need its holder's consent (§ 1071 BGB)                                      |
| =ManagementShare=      | the partner's management rights are split between the partner and f's holder                    |
| =CoDetermines=         | f's holder can decisively co-determine the partnership's affairs                                 |
| =EquatedWithPartner=   | f's holder is to be treated like a partner (§ 108 HGB)                                           |
| =CourtNeedsInfo=       | the register court needs to know of f to check applications and resolutions                      |
| =InfoInterest=         | legal dealings have a (general) interest in knowing of f                                         |
| =DelayedFilings=       | applications to register facts like f are often filed late                                       |
| =DisposalPower=        | f's holder has power of disposal over the interest                                               |
| =ProceduralParty=      | creditors must sue f's holder (party by virtue of office)                                        |
| =LikeExecutorship=     | f shares the features for which the BGH held the executorship registrable                       |
| =EmolumentsRight=      | f is a right to take emoluments (§§ 1030 (1), 1068 (2) BGB)                                      |
| =PartnerStatus=        | f's holder becomes a partner                                                                     |
| =UniformVote=          | the vote attached to the interest f burdens can only be exercised uniformly                      |
| =CreatedWithoutPartnership= | f can be created without the partnership's involvement or knowledge                         |
| =OnlyDamages=          | acting without f's holder's consent gives rise only to damages                                    |
| =Powerless=            | helper: f's holder has no means of influence on the partners' decisions (below)                  |
| =Unlike=               | helper: f lacks every feature for which the executorship was held registrable (below)            |

#+begin_src fspy

declare Liability, Representation, VotesDayToDay, ConsentConstitutive, Consent1071 : Fact -> bool.
declare ManagementShare, CoDetermines, EquatedWithPartner, CourtNeedsInfo, InfoInterest : Fact -> bool.
declare DelayedFilings, DisposalPower, ProceduralParty, LikeExecutorship : Fact -> bool.
declare EmolumentsRight, PartnerStatus, UniformVote, CreatedWithoutPartnership, OnlyDamages : Fact -> bool.
declare Powerless, Unlike : Fact -> bool.
#+end_src

* General principles (propositional)

| Atom                     | Reads                                                                                         |
|--------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| =LiabilityOnly=          | the register is to show the liability position only                                           |
| =InfoSuffices=           | a general interest of legal dealings in a piece of information suffices for its registration  |
| =ClarityPrecedence=      | clarity of the register (as a rule) takes precedence over the interest in comprehensive information |
| =DelayDefeatsInterest=   | that such applications are filed late defeats the interest in their registration               |
| =DeclaratoryRegistrable= | facts whose entry has no constitutive effect can be registrable (§ 15 (2) HGB)                 |
| =LimitedPartnerRepresents= | a limited partner represents the partnership                                                |

* What no court disputes (declared, hence strict)

A negative fact is a denial (=deny NAME : (P).=, used with =moxia=), not
a declared =~P=. A counterargument then refutes P directly. The
=cut (~P) x= lemma pattern is avoided because of an AIDA unfolding bug
(=tests/iris/fixtures/bug_unfold_negation_lemma.fspy=, tasks.org).

- Statutes, the BGH holdings the courts take as given, and the content
  of the principles above.
- Whatever a court /argues/ is formalised in that court's file, not
  here.

#+begin_src fspy
# §§ 106 (2), 162 (1) HGB do not list the usufruct.
deny s_162 : (ProvidedByStatute [usufruct]).
# § 1071 BGB.
declare s_1071 : (Consent1071 [usufruct]).
# §§ 1030 (1), 1068 (2) BGB: the usufruct is a right to take emoluments.
declare s_1030 : (EmolumentsRight [usufruct]).
# BGH NJW 1999, 571: the usufructuary does not become a partner.
deny bgh_partner : (PartnerStatus [usufruct]).
# § 170 HGB: a limited partner does not represent the partnership.
deny s_170 : (LimitedPartnerRepresents).
# BGH, 14.2.2012, II ZB 15/11: the executorship is registrable, and the
# executor holds power of disposal, liability effect, procedural position.
declare bgh_exec_reg : (Registrable [executorship]).
declare bgh_exec_disposal : (DisposalPower [executorship]).
declare bgh_exec_liab : (Liability [executorship]).
declare bgh_exec_proc : (ProceduralParty [executorship]).
# BGH NJW 1998, 1071 and BGH 24.2.2015: of interest, yet not registrable.
declare bgh_deputy_interest : (InfoInterest [deputy_note]).
deny bgh_deputy_reg : (Registrable [deputy_note]).
declare bgh_gmbh_interest : (InfoInterest [gmbh_executorship]).
deny bgh_gmbh_reg : (Registrable [gmbh_executorship]).
# § 15 (2) HGB presupposes registrable facts without constitutive entry.
declare s_15 : (DeclaratoryRegistrable).
# BGH, 14.2.2012, II ZB 15/11 (Rn. 21): the register is also to inform about
# the persons taking part in the partnership, not only about liability.
deny bgh_participants : (LiabilityOnly).
# The analogy schema: what holds for the executorship holds for a fact
# that shares the features for which it held.
declare analogy_exec : (Registrable [executorship] -> LikeExecutorship [usufruct] -> Registrable [usufruct]).
#+end_src

* The meta-norm (IRIS draft § 3.2) as every court applies it

A fact is registrable if the statute provides for its entry, or if there
is a need of legal dealings. Conversely, the principle of restraint
excludes it. That principle is a helper with one denial per necessary
condition (analysis.org §4):
- The exclusion requires that the statute does not provide for the
  entry.
- It also requires that there is no need. Whoever claims the need has
  to establish it. The counterargument's site on the need is owed:
  =next=.

The content of the general principles enters as their instances at the
facts concerned.

#+begin_src fspy
declare m_stat : (ProvidedByStatute [usufruct] -> Registrable [usufruct]).
declare m_need : (Need [usufruct] -> Registrable [usufruct]).
declare m_need_deputy : (Need [deputy_note] -> Registrable [deputy_note]).
declare m_need_gmbh : (Need [gmbh_executorship] -> Registrable [gmbh_executorship]).
declare m_excl : (Excluded [usufruct] -> ~Registrable [usufruct]).
deny dn_excl_stat : (Excluded [usufruct] - ~ProvidedByStatute [usufruct]).
deny dn_excl_need : (Excluded [usufruct] - ~Need [usufruct]).
# The principles' content, at the facts concerned.
declare p_info : (InfoSuffices -> InfoInterest [usufruct] -> Need [usufruct]).
declare p_info_deputy : (InfoSuffices -> InfoInterest [deputy_note] -> Need [deputy_note]).
declare p_info_gmbh : (InfoSuffices -> InfoInterest [gmbh_executorship] -> Need [gmbh_executorship]).
deny p_liab_only : (LiabilityOnly - (Need [usufruct] -> Liability [usufruct])).

declare g_liab : (Liability [usufruct] -> Need [usufruct]).

* Two more helpers

- *No means of influence* (=Powerless=). München II.2 b)-c) and Köln
  Rn. 5 deny co-determination by denying, one after another, the means
  of influence claimed.
  - The helper makes that an open list. Its necessary conditions are
    "no vote of their own" and "no constitutive consent".
  - Whoever claims influence owes the means: the sites are owed.
- *Unlike the executorship* (=Unlike=). München II.2 f) reads the
  BGH's holding as resting on power of disposal, liability effect and
  procedural position. The analogy fails if the usufruct has none of
  them; each is a necessary condition of the difference.

AI NOTE: =p_liab_only= states a necessary condition of the need, under
the principle as its guard: if the register is to show liability only,
a need without liability is excluded (=deny LiabilityOnly - (Need ->
Liability)=, the subtraction on the context side, =analysis.org=,
decisions of 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-17).
- A first form with the negated premise =~Liability= made Fellowship
  prove that premise as a λ typed =Liability -> false=. That spelled the
  statement two ways, and it forced the expanded type check of the whole
  combined term (62,000 AST nodes instead of 1,800; see =README.org=).
- The contraposed implication that replaced it ("a need requires
  liability", used by modus tollens) became the subtraction on
  2026-11-16, and the denied guarded form on 2026-11-17. Neither form
  has a negated premise.
