# DC (Debate calculus)

Files
- argument.py
  - Argument: a recorded or registered argument. execute() replays its instructions against Fellowship and reads back its proof term; open sites, enrichment and rendering.
- discipline.py
  - The argument discipline of the pure level: linear arguments, dual-linear counterarguments, no debate scaffold (tasks.org, aida-argument-lj-restriction); `violations(body, anti)`, enforced by wrap/registry.py at `dixi`.
- debate.py
  - Debate, Move: debates as named, ordered, scoped selections of the document's arguments; the verb checks; unfold_debate.
- debate_graph.py
  - Debate-graph compilation (debate-graph-spec.org, layers 2-3): DebateGraph, the document graph. Since 2026-10-15 by the horn rule: a binder's derivation has every horn of its command as a source, a horn that is a declared axiom or a progress node is an edge of its own, the spine (what carries the binder's own variable) gets none. First order (2026-10-17, aida-first-order-reduction; the horn rule applied plainly since 2026-10-22): an instantiation (`ConsFO`), an existential's introduction (`TermsPairFO`) and elimination on the spine are steps of it, off the spine horns with their quantified statement; a universal introduction (`LamdaFO`) is a horn like a lambda; a horn whose statement or content mentions a first-order variable bound in the body is not exposed (step 0, kept: aida-referents-open-horns). Discourse referents (2026-10-20, aida-discourse-referents): an argument body is compiled with every proposition in the context it stands in (`contextualise`: the argument's referent environment and the binders above it), and a node's key is the statement in that context (`context_key`): entries the statement mentions are referents, numbered as bound variables, the others contribute their sorts. Weakening edges (`DebateGraph.weaken`) join a statement to it in every larger context, on both sides. A referent target owes a witness for each referent (`require_witnesses`). Context entries (`referents.Entry`) are binder entries (a variable a binder introduces, a parameter) or witness entries (an argument's open choice, `elim [?w]` with `?w` not in its conclusion): a witness entry is rigid and its argument's in a key (`referents.rigid`), and the place where an argument names it owes "a witness for ?w of A", or, for `!w`, presumes the sort node `exists x:foo, true` (`_choice`, `sort_individuals`: a declared constant proves it strictly). Instances (`DebateGraph.instantiate`, aida-referents-instantiation): a statement with variables covers every node that is it with terms for them (`core/dc/matching.py`, one-sided); each argument deriving or contesting it is copied to the instance with its parts (`Edge.generic`, `Edge.inst`), and markers an argument asserts of its own conclusion go along; `instantiations` holds the nearest pairs for display.
- unfold.py
  - Unfolding: document graph -> debate term, for an issue or an argument entrypoint; every exposed node (an axiom applied, a lemma, a lambda) becomes the debate about its statement where it stands. Discourse referents: a site is captured by a binder of its literal statement, in any context; otherwise, under binders, it is the statement in the context at that use, framed with that context, and its supporters and attackers come from every node of the statement in a smaller context and from the instances copied there, each matched against the statement as written at that use (`_lineups`, one-sided matching), substituted before capture, its own first-order binders renamed where their names are taken. An argument's open choice is never lined up: where the argument names it, a witness scaffold at the quantified statement has the open choice as its root and as alternatives the constants some statement is about there and the variables binders introduced in scope, the innermost on top (`witness_scaffold`, `_alternatives`); an open choice is renamed where its name is taken. Copies placed with a substitution and the alternatives are marked `instantiated`, so the strict phase reads their edges off the term (`strict.instance_edges`). The unfolded term's propositions carry their contexts, witness entries included, so labels and strictness read the right statements. Open sites are numbered `?1`, `?2`, ....
